The exterior is where bids go wrong. The apps skip it.

Wall area is easy. A sun-baked south side with peeling at every drip edge, a porch roof you have to walk, and a second story nobody wants to ladder — that's where a September job eats your profit.

How most estimating apps handle prep

  • Prep hours: ____
  • Height assumed at 10 ft per story
  • Ladders, walk boards and lifts not priced
  • Siding condition not asked
  • Generic production rates, or none until you've tracked a season

How Backroll handles it

  • Each side priced on its own
  • Condition on a 1–4 scale, with photos of what each grade looks like
  • Access priced: ladder, walk board, lift
  • Prep tasks checked off and turned into hours
  • Starts with real exterior rates, then learns yours from finished jobs

Questions

Does it replace Jobber or DripJobs?

No. Backroll does one thing — exterior estimates that hold up. Keep your scheduling and invoicing where they are; Backroll hands the job off to them.

What about interiors?

Exteriors first, because that's where estimates go most wrong and where the tools are weakest. Interiors may follow.

Where do the starting rates come from?

Real exterior production rates from a decade of painting in Wisconsin. They're a starting point — Backroll replaces them with your crew's actual numbers as you finish jobs.

Does the AI set my price?

No. The math is plain, visible formulas using your rates. AI only helps with things like writing the scope on your proposal. You approve every number.
